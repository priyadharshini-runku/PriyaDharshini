Academia–Industry Skill Mapping & Readiness Engine

THE CHALLENGE & CONCEPT

Colleges often educate students without real-time alignment to changing industry requirements. SkillBridge is a student-designed platform concept connecting students, faculty, placement officers, and companies to systematically identify job-specific skill gaps and improve graduate employability.

CORE ARCHITECTURAL FEATURES
01. Skill Mapping: Live mapping of curriculum coursework to targeted technical roles.
02. Readiness Assessment: Quantitative benchmarks on technical & soft skill proficiencies.
03. Certification Tracking: Verification hub for validated industry credentials.
04. Gap Analysis: Actionable learning roadmaps to bridge critical deficiencies.
